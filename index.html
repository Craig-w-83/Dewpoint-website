What we do

We provide evidence, not guesswork.

We operate thermal-camera drones at dawn and dusk to detect animals when their natural body warmth has greater contrast against the cool ground. Every heat signature is checked across multiple frames to confirm its legitimacy, then mapped to GPS coordinates. This process provides real, usable data you can hand to a pest controller, a compliance officer, or utilise within your own biosecurity management plan.

Thermal detection

Animals show up against cool ground even in thick cover or low light.

Dawn & dusk surveys

Flights are timed to dawn and dusk, when the temperature contrast is greatest.

GPS-mapped evidence

Every detection is plotted to within 10 metres and logged with a timestamp.

What we detect

Every service works the same way: aerial detection over your property, delivered as an evidence-grade report.

Core service
Feral detection

Thermal surveys at dawn and dusk that locate feral pigs, deer and foxes across your property. Every confirmed detection is mapped to GPS coordinates and delivered as an evidence-grade report you can act on. This is our core service, used three ways: baseline surveys before control work begins, verification flights that measure whether it worked, and ongoing monitoring that catches reinvasion early.

Landholder property add-ons

Fence line, dam and water-point inspections, plus stock location and muster support, completed while we're already over your property. Bolt them onto any survey for a fraction of the cost of a standalone visit.

Who it's for
Landholders & farmers

Know what's impacting your property! Get the evidence you need for effective pest control, fence planning and property management. That means confirmed numbers and mapped locations you can hand to a controller, use to justify spend, or keep as a record of what's moving through your country.

Councils & Natural Resource Management (NRM) bodies

Compliance-grade survey data for biosecurity programs, catchment plans and pest reporting: defensible counts and mapped locations you can cite in funding applications, control-program reviews and community reporting.

Pest controllers

Know where activity is concentrated before you plan your service. Our surveys give you likely numbers and activity areas across a property, planning-grade evidence to maximise efficiencies and provide greater value to the end client.

How it works
01
Book a flight window

Tell us the property and target species. We confirm a dawn or dusk window that fits to your schedule and requirements.

02
We fly

The flight window is set to suit the service booked. Thermal detection flies at dawn or dusk when heat contrast is greatest, while property inspections can run through the day.

03
GPS-mapped report

Where possible, estimated counts are given along with species, locations mapped to within 10 metres, with detailed documentation.

04
You act on it

With the right evidence, comes confidence in decision making. Dewpoint provides you with the information you need for the next step in property and land management.

What you'll receive

Every survey includes a mapped report: estimated detection counts by species, GPS-plotted locations, thermal capture stills and a methodology summary, ready to attach to a compliance file or hand straight to a pest controller.
